# P3 career pages — 142/142 subjects (2026-11-12 → 2026-11-14, 7 waves of 2 subagents)

## What shipped
- **142 careers pages** (one per subject) at `subjects/<id>/careers/` + a `/careers` index,
  built on data files `astro-website/src/data/careers/<id>.json` rendered by
  `src/components/CareersPage.astro`; route wrappers generated by
  `pipeline/gen_career_routes.py` (idempotent; also removes stale routes).
- Each subject page links to its careers page (conditional chip in `SubjectPage.astro`).
- **~620 roles** across the 142 pages (3–6 per subject), each carrying: exact O*NET title +
  O*NET-SOC code (linked to onetonline.org), US median pay with month/year, projected
  growth with window, typical entry education, a grounded description, and a sentence
  tying the role to that subject's actual syllabus units; BLS OOH page cited per role.
- EURES portal linked on every page (no data claims — portal unreachable from the build machine).

## Anti-hallucination toolchain
- `pipeline/packs/onet-31-occupation-data.txt` — O*NET 31.0 Database occupation table
  (code/title/description), extracted from the official N-Triples download, CC BY 4.0.
- `pipeline/validate_careers.py` — hard-fails any role whose title+code pair is not an
  exact match in the pack; checks OOH URL shape, pay ($ + year), growth (% + window),
  entry, prose lengths and banned characters. 142/142 PASS at ship time.
- Pay/growth/entry figures came from bls.gov-restricted WebSearch per role (OOH pages are
  bot-blocked to direct fetches; search-indexed figures used instead), quoting the values
  as returned (May 2025 medians, 2025–2035 windows).

## Verification performed
- Validator green (142/142) after every wave; central re-run before each commit.
- Per-wave spot checks against fresh bls.gov searches (13 roles sampled across waves:
  accountants, neurologists, lawyers, interpreters, epidemiologists, civil engineers,
  dental hygienists, welders, petroleum engineers, veterinarians, radiologic
  technologists, pest control workers, brickmasons) — all matched the cited OOH pages exactly.
- `npm run build` green per wave; final build 885 pages, all 142 routes present in dist,
  all 142 subject pages carry the careers link.
- Roles without verifiable BLS OOH figures were dropped and replaced (recorded per wave
  by the subagents; e.g. Music Therapists / Art Therapists have no standalone OOH profile
  and use allied verified roles instead).

## Remaining
Nothing in the roadmap. (Optional future: add careers pages to the search index;
